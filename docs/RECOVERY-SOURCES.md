# Recovery guidance and official help

Reviewed 21 November 2026. Recheck contact details and platform instructions before submission and each public release. This is static, curated guidance; it is not scraped news, AI-generated recovery advice, a police integration or legal advice.

## Official UAE help

- [Abu Dhabi Police Aman contact page](https://srv.adpolice.gov.ae/en/aman/Pages/contactus.aspx): UAE phone 8002626, SMS 2828, international +971 8002626. The app shows the numbers offline and opens only the phone dialer or blank SMS composer on an explicit tap. It does not compose/send a report or promise a callback.
- [Aman FAQ](https://srv.adpolice.gov.ae/en/aman/pages/qa.aspx): Aman receives security information, not formal criminal complaints; formal reports can be made at a police station. Emergencies use 999, not Aman. These distinctions remain visible.
- [Abu Dhabi Police cybercrime awareness guidance](https://abudhabipolice.gov.ae/Media-Center/News/2023/06/2006202302): report fraudulent approaches through Aman; victims of fraud should contact their bank and nearest police station.
- [UAE Government emergency guidance](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/justice-safety-and-the-law/handling-emergencies): police emergency number 999 within the UAE.

Bank contact details depend on the user's bank, so the app deliberately uses its real app or an already-trusted number rather than inventing a universal bank hotline. Payment recovery is never guaranteed. The emergency option is explicitly labelled and user initiated.

## Blocking/reporting instructions

- [WhatsApp Help Center](https://faq.whatsapp.com/1142481766359885/): sender/contact details, block/report choices, confirmation and recent-message sharing. The official indexed help content was available; direct fetching was restricted. Recheck the actual installed app during device testing.
- [Apple iPhone User Guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/report-spam-and-block-senders-iph3f94d910d/ios): sender blocking and junk/spam reporting. Official indexed content was available; the direct page returned a rate-limit response during review. Reporting availability depends on OS/carrier; blocking is checked separately.
- [Google Messages blocking](https://support.google.com/messages/answer/7029021?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid&hl=en) and [spam reporting](https://support.google.com/messages/answer/9061432?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid&hl=en): long-press conversation, block/report, Spam & blocked destination.

The app's drawings are original schematic phone panels using existing Ionicons and theme tokens, not copied screenshots or official branded assets. Instructions are paraphrased. They warn that reporting may remove the conversation or send recent messages to the provider, and tell users to preserve evidence first. They do not visit suspicious links, operate other apps, or submit reports.

## General recovery background and limitations

[FTC consumer recovery guidance](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-do-if-you-were-scammed) and the existing [NCSC phishing guidance](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/phishing-scams/what-to-do) inform password, payment and device-support advice. US-specific reporting/legal claims are not presented as UAE advice. Official help shown to users is UAE-focused.

Recovery answers are transient React state, not stored or sent to AI. Unknown answers show conditional guidance rather than reassuring users. All five questions require an explicit answer, with Not sure available. Payment/card questions share one recovery topic. Completion checkboxes are reminders, never evidence of restored security. No result certifies an account or phone as safe.

Automated browser checks cannot establish native dialer/SMS handoff, Android hardware Back, VoiceOver/TalkBack behavior or exact current third-party app menus. These require device checks. Do not place test calls to emergency or police services.
